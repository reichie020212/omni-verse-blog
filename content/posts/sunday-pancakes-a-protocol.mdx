She insists on whisking. I insist on measuring. We have arrived at a stable equilibrium.

## The agreement, as ratified

1 cup flour. 1 cup milk. 1 egg. A pinch of salt she pours from a height because it is funny. Butter in the pan, never in the batter — this was her amendment, and she was right.
